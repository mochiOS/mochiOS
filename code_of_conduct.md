# mochiOS Code of Conduct
An operating system abstracts a wide variety of hardware and provides a safe and comfortable environment for applications.
Consequently, its implementation is diverse, and it is only natural that the people involved in its development also
possess a wide range of backgrounds, opinions, and uniqueness.

The goal of mochiOS is to collect and publish information useful for OS development, and to facilitate interaction among developers.
To achieve this, it is essential that all participants respect one another and remain constructive.
We hereby establish this Code of Conduct as a guide to reaching that goal.

All individuals involved in events hosted by mochiOS maintainers, or in communities managed by mochiOS maintainers,
are required to comply with the contents of this Code of Conduct.

mochiOS maintainers have a responsibility to speak up and request that any participant engaging in behavior that violates
this Code of Conduct cease such actions. At the same time, the appropriate conduct of each participant is vital.
Participants who persist in behavior that undermines the safety of the community in violation of this Code of Conduct
may be subject to measures such as being banned from this community or its hosted events.

### Expected Behavior (Please do these)

- Respect different opinions, perspectives, and experiences, and welcome mutual differences.
- Actively voice constructive opinions and share knowledge widely.
- Act without fear of failure, accept failure, and learn from it.
- Always treat each other as equals with respect.

### Unacceptable Behavior (Do not do these)
- Disparaging or slandering specific individuals, organizations, products, or projects.
- Offensive, inappropriate, or unnecessary remarks regarding gender, gender identity and expression,
  sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, or age.
- Any form of intimidation, bullying, or harassment toward others.
